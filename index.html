Company Profile

Team Phoenix Technology Limited (T.P.T.), founded in 2006, is a leading Hong Kong-based system solutions provider. With a strong track record in system integration and deep expertise in infrastructure solutions, we empower businesses to thrive in a competitive marketplace. Our mission is to deliver end-to-end infrastructure, fully integrated systems, high-performance applications, and strategic consulting—helping our customers gain a decisive edge in every aspect of their operations.

Vision Statement

Our vision is to be the best business IT solutions company serving the small and medium business community. To achieve this, we prioritize serving people over selling products. To us, service means following through, treating the customer with respect, actively listening, and recommending only what is of real value to our customers; this is how we grow our clientele.

We take pride in leveraging our experience and business partnerships to deliver sophisticated solutions that are stable and integrate new technologies. We value innovation, integrity, and a strong work ethic in our employees and business partners. We encourage creativity and leadership in every team member.

We want to create a workplace where everyone feels comfortable sharing their ideas without fear and where everyone is encouraged to laugh often and out loud. This environment of growth benefits not only our valued customers, but also our entire community.
